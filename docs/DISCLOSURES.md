# Research evidence, review scope and text fidelity

DueCare is educational red-teaming research. It examines recorded model responses to source research questions, adapted safeguarding scenarios and controlled tasks. The public examples support study of model behavior, including deficient answers and failed decisions. Applying a result to a worker's situation requires qualified human review, current local evidence and a separate assessment of the proposed use. Public material contains reviewed research text and numeric observations; identifying personal data stays outside the release.

## Keep the comparison valid

The main report evaluates 50 actual responses to complete source prompts across original-question and evidence-assisted conditions. All were read in full by automated assistant reviewers; the two arms used separate reviewers, followed by a shared criterion-independence review and selected cross-arm checks. A useful protective suggestion can coexist with facilitation or legal error. Independent human, worker-informed and legal adjudication of this cohort remains open. Jev's response grades are released separately from the main text reviews.

Three representations must remain distinct:

1. **Verbatim source:** exact original text with its provenance and supplied labels. The private archive preserves the source bytes; validating each label is a separate task.
2. **Bounded derivative:** an adapted prompt with its own protocol identifier and transformation record. Shortening, abstraction or redaction can alter intent and difficulty. Results apply to the version that the model received.
3. **Controlled policy task:** a scenario with an expected outcome under a stated policy. The reference expresses that policy's assumptions. Independent case adjudication requires its own evidence and reviewers.

Generation and grading use the same case text and evidence. Give each rewrite a new version and assess it against that version's reference. Changes to actor, intent, consent, chronology, money, debt, threats, control, jurisdiction or uncertainty require review. Preserve the original digest and a transformation record. Review the effect of whitespace, translation, encoding and paraphrase on meaning as well as on the software interface.

The style-equivalence tests stipulate equivalence under a narrow screening policy. Their ties measure compliance with that policy. Reader-specific usefulness, accessibility and overall answer quality have separate evaluation criteria.

The current corpus primarily evaluates English. Native-language, legal, affected-worker and independent human review remain open validation work. Repeated votes from one model family measure that family's consistency; corroboration requires independent evidence.

## Data handling

Public exports use reviewed examples and numeric evidence. Identifying details, real worker records, private attachments, credentials and restricted raw outputs stay in their authorized storage. Encryption protects stored bytes. Recipient approval, evaluator review and operating-system controls address access and retention during use. See [full-text evaluation](FULL_TEXT_EVALUATION.md).

Operationally enabling legacy material remains private. The public release keeps reference errors, missing outputs, refusals, disagreements and failed tests visible. Each finding applies to its declared tasks and model configuration. Assessment of a real cross-border case belongs with qualified practitioners using current evidence.
