# Presentation variation protocol

Quality, register, format, length and specificity are separate design dimensions. A terse answer can be excellent; a fluent, technical answer can be wrong. Colloquial language is not a proxy for low quality. Specificity may use supplied facts but cannot invent detail.

| Dimension | Values |
|---|---|
| Requested quality | worst, bad, neutral, good, best |
| Length | 25-55, 56-95, 96-175, 280-450 words |
| Format | prose, bullets, memo, explained screening, Q&A, table, JSON, assessor-reviewer dialogue |
| Register | very simple, plain, colloquial, professional, technical, dense specialist |
| Specificity | general but supported, linked to case facts, explicit evidence distinctions |
| Prose | compact, flowing, varied sentence length |

The supplement contains 72 concern, control and information-gap cases across 24 mechanism families. Each case has 12 presentation profiles: four lengths and three variants. Every register appears twice per case, and all eight formats occur. The same 12 profiles are reused for all five requested quality tiers: 72 x 12 x 5 = 4,320 Tactical requests. The ordinary best-effort baseline for each comparison model contains 864 requests.

The design is sampled, not the full Cartesian product of all dimensions. Presentation assignments are reproducible; the hosted adapter does not expose a generation seed. Sampling temperatures for Tactical are 0.35, 0.65 and 0.95. Cloud baselines use their declared fixed decoding settings. Historic source examples are preserved, not retroactively rewritten into this protocol.

The public version 2 comparison fixture contains 1,728 requests: 864 expected ties and 864 altered-conclusion controls, all shown in both positions. Judges receive the generic screening policy but not the expected outcome or a statement that the pair is equivalent. Position stability is reported separately from correctness. Always answering “tie” scores 50%, not 100%, on this mixed fixture.

An earlier private version explicitly asserted pair equivalence. It remains a labelled, cued diagnostic and is excluded from claims about unbiased judging. The corrected comparison fixture is a separate protocol; it does not modify already-running candidate generation. References remain constructed, policy-conditioned expectations, not independently human-adjudicated preferences.

The original 20,080-candidate campaign remains frozen and separate. It has 251 source prompts, 248 distinct bounded fact summaries, four length bands and four variants per requested tier and length. Its eight-style pool predates the supplement's explicit register and specificity dimensions. Results from the two designs must not be pooled without retaining their protocol IDs.

All new low-tier examples are bounded abstract errors or omissions, not instructions for exploitation, coercion, concealment, retaliation or surveillance. Independent judges do not see the requested tier or style assignment. Refusals, quality-tier mismatches, duplicates and length misses are outcomes, not automatically successful samples.
