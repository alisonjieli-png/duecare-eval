# Review DueCare evidence

Give the reader a claim they can trace to a defined population, executed task and saved observation.

## Establish the evidence scope

Locate the DueCare checkout in the user-selected workspace through `pyproject.toml`, `src/duecare_eval/` and repository instructions. The public project is `duecare-eval-public`. Read the current status or publication handoff when available, plus `docs/EXTENDING_DUECARE.md` for extension-pack results. A dated public snapshot and a continuing private campaign can both be valid records of different times.

Identify the question being reviewed, protocol versions, source cases, model configurations, assessment layer and capture time. Use manifests, requested-ID inventories and canonical journals. Deduplicate copied journals and reused observations before totaling calls. Count archived prompts, authored cases and actual provider attempts separately.

Show requested, attempted, transport-completed, usable and assessed counts. State who performed any independent validation. Keep errors, truncations, unattempted slots and unknown outcomes in coverage. A model access check proves access for that request; a fixture tests code behavior. Neither supplies a benchmark-quality observation.

## Check meaning as well as arithmetic

Reproduce numerical claims offline from the declared snapshot. Use the intersection of matched case and question IDs for shared-task comparisons, and show omitted coverage. Explain whether recognition was elicited by a direct question or appeared in spontaneous advice. Changes in prompt, serving controls, parser, rubric or evidence packet need their own labels when interpreting a difference.

Keep raw outputs, extracted values and policy-derived guards separate. Evidence support, confidence and action urgency answer different questions. Several indicators or protective actions may apply at once. A source list shows which material was supplied; actual source use requires evidence from the response. In tool-using evaluations, distinguish an intended action from execution and a verified postcondition.

For substantive prose review, read the full answer and use the versioned rubric. Record exact supporting quotes and offsets, or an explicit absence after a full read. Recognition, practical help, worker agency, factual calibration and harmful facilitation can coexist. Apply harm flags and critical caps only under their stated evidence rules; preserve the raw grade and cap reasons. Citation wording or a trafficking keyword alone proves neither correct reasoning nor a legal conclusion.

Scope legal checks to the sources, facts and jurisdiction available. Mark unsupported claims for verification where the evidence cannot settle them. Benign and mixed cases deserve useful ordinary advice and careful treatment of unknowns. Model judges and model-authored references remain identifiable assessment sources, including same-model or same-family overlap.

## Deliver the review

Lead with supported findings, then material gaps and the next bounded test. Link the relevant manifest, observation bundle, reproduction command and rubric. State denominators beside scores and distinguish agreement with provisional references from independently established correctness.

Keep restricted responses and identifiable personal material out of public artifacts. Use reviewed short excerpts and allowlisted numeric fields where appropriate. A review request authorizes inspection and reporting; changes, new inference and publication follow the user's requested scope. A publication task also requires the repository's reproduction, export and rendered-artifact checks. Preserve earlier snapshots while recording revised evidence in a new version.
